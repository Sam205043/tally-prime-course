📋 Tally Voucher Guide

Kaunsi transaction mein kaunsa voucher — with SEML examples

F4
Contra Voucher
Cash ↔ Bank Transfers
⌄
Kab Use Karo / Kab Nahi
✓Bank se cash nikaalna — ATM withdrawal
✓Cash bank mein deposit karna
✓One bank to another bank transfer
✗Customer se payment receive — F6 use karo
✗Supplier ko payment — F5 use karo
SEML Example Entry
Dr Cash A/c 50,000 Cr Bank A/c (HDFC) 50,000 [Narration: Cash withdrawn from HDFC Bank for office use]
F5
Payment Voucher
Outgoing Payments — Cash ya Bank se
⌄
Kab Use Karo / Kab Nahi
✓Salary payment karna — Dr Salary, Cr Bank
✓Rent payment karna
✓Supplier ko payment — udhar chukana
✓TDS deposit — government ko payment
✗Sales entry — F8 use karo
✗Cash ko bank mein dalna — F4 use karo
SEML Example Entry
Dr Salary A/c 2,40,000 Cr Bank A/c (HDFC) 2,40,000 [Narration: Salary paid for July 2025]
F6
Receipt Voucher
Incoming Payments — Cash ya Bank mein
⌄
Kab Use Karo / Kab Nahi
✓Customer se payment receive karna
✓Capital investment — owner ne paisa diya
✓Loan liya — bank se amount aai
✓Advance receipt from customer
✗Goods purchase return — Credit Note use karo
✗Bank se cash nikaalna — F4 use karo
SEML Example Entry
Dr Bank A/c (HDFC) 3,50,000 Cr Tata Steel Ltd (Debtor) 3,50,000 [Narration: Payment received from Tata Steel against Invoice #201]
F7
Journal Voucher
Non-Cash Adjustments & Provisions
⌄
Kab Use Karo / Kab Nahi
✓Depreciation entry — machinery pe
✓TDS payable entry — kaat liya par deposit nahi
✓Provision for expenses — month-end
✓Outstanding salary provision
✓Prepaid expense adjustment
✗Cash ya bank transactions — F4/F5/F6 use karo
SEML Example Entry
Dr Depreciation A/c 75,000 Cr Machinery A/c 75,000 [Narration: Depreciation on Plant & Machinery @15% for FY 2025-26]
F8
Sales Voucher
Sales Invoice / Delivery
⌄
Kab Use Karo / Kab Nahi
✓Customer ko goods/services bechna — GST ke saath
✓Sales invoice banana — B2B aur B2C dono
✓Export sales entry
✓Service invoice — consulting, fabrication
✗Purchase entry — F9 use karo
✗Customer se payment — F6 use karo
SEML Example Entry
Dr Tata Steel Ltd (Debtor) 4,13,000 Cr Steel Gate Sales 3,50,000 Cr CGST Payable 31,500 Cr SGST Payable 31,500 [Narration: Tax Invoice #SEML/2025/001 — Steel Gate supply with 18% GST]
F9
Purchase Voucher
Purchase Invoice / Goods Receipt
⌄
Kab Use Karo / Kab Nahi
✓Supplier ka bill record karna — raw material, goods
✓GST purchase with ITC claim
✓Service purchase — contractor bill
✓Import purchase entry
✗Sales entry — F8 use karo
✗Supplier ko payment — F5 use karo
SEML Example Entry
Dr Raw Steel A/c 1,00,000 Dr CGST Input 9,000 Dr SGST Input 9,000 Cr JSW Steel Ltd (Creditor) 1,18,000 [Narration: Raw steel purchased from JSW Steel, Invoice #JSW/1234 with 18% GST]
